## Use of identifiers

`project_doc.txt` has no formal requirement identifiers. The `PROV-*` identifiers below are stable
internal identifiers for its requirements. The approved decision addendum defines independently
traceable `DEC-*` requirements with exact thresholds, planned milestones/paths and verification. The
product owner confirmed this combined baseline is complete. Status entries cite executed evidence;
uncited planned paths and tests remain forecasts, not evidence.

The `DEC-*` tables in `docs/decision-addendum-2026-08-18.md` are part of this traceability baseline
rather than a narrative-only reference. ADR-0001 through ADR-0005 record the architecture
consequences; the addendum preserves the independently testable product-owner requirements.

Status vocabulary: `Blocked` means a decision or authoritative requirement is missing; `Planned`
means the requirement can be planned but has no implementation evidence; `Verified` may be used only
after the cited command/test was executed successfully.
